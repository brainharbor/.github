# brAInharbor

**Self-hosted AI for small businesses, in Hungarian.** One Linux machine, your data stays on it, no monthly cloud bill.

- [brainharbor](https://github.com/brainharbor/brainharbor): the installer package. Ollama, Open WebUI and Caddy with HTTPS, Hungarian defaults, preflight check, backup and restore.
- [Hungarian test suite](https://github.com/brainharbor/brainharbor/tree/main/tests/hu): measure any local model or prompt on real office tasks.
- [Blog](https://brainharbor.net): test results and design decisions, in Hungarian.

Open source under the MIT licence. Questions and bug reports are welcome in the [issues](https://github.com/brainharbor/brainharbor/issues).
